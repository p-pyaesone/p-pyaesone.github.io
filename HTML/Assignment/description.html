Early Life and Education

Rosé was born in Auckland, New Zealand, and raised in Melbourne, Australia. She developed an interest in music at a young age and learned to sing and play musical instruments, including the guitar and piano. In 2012, she auditioned for YG Entertainment in Australia and was selected as a trainee. She later moved to South Korea to pursue her dream of becoming a professional singer.
